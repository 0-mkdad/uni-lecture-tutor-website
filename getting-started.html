Getting started

Learn how to start a university study session, practise independently, and resume with a portable checkpoint.

From your first lecture to independent practice, in five steps.

  1. Check access in ChatGPT

    Look for Uni Lecture Tutor in your available apps or skills. Public listing availability is not confirmed on this website. The publisher does not charge a separate tutor fee; ChatGPT plan and regional limits apply.

  2. Attach your course material

    Invoke the tutor and submit one or more accessible lecture files. A separate written start prompt is optional. The tutor is designed to begin the first supported objective in the same response, without waiting to inspect every file. An attachment not submitted or a tutor not invoked cannot start the workflow. You can also paste a permitted excerpt and request a specific task.

  3. Choose your learning language

    Choose the explanation, notes and exam languages separately if you wish. By default notes follow the lecture language, and exam prompts follow the notes language. Review the course map and prerequisites. Explicit requests override the default workflow.

  4. Practise before revealing the answer

    Work through one concept at a time. Try the practice question independently, request a hint if needed, and use feedback to identify what to revisit.

  5. Save a portable checkpoint

    Request or accept an offered portable checkpoint and save it yourself. In a new conversation invoke the tutor and submit the checkpoint with relevant lecture files. Imported history is treated as reported until reconciled with accessible evidence. A valid JSON structure does not prove mastery or restore inaccessible files.

Know the limits

AI can make mistakes. Check definitions, page references, formulas and answers against your course material. Interactive controls and local verification depend on the host. Coverage is not mastery. Follow your institution’s rules.

Learning choices and exports

You can request guided or continuous teaching, brief or deep explanation, direct answers, review, independent practice, a practice exam or notes. Ask for the study script, formula sheet, glossary, exam, separate solutions, flashcards, error notebook or JSON checkpoint. File generation depends on the host; copyable text is the fallback.
