Support

Get help with lecture files, study checkpoints and issues with Uni Lecture Tutor.

Email support ChatGPT Help Center

Unreadable PDF

Try a version with selectable text or share a short permitted excerpt. Mention both the printed and PDF page numbers if they differ.

Resume or report an error

Paste your checkpoint to resume. To report an error, send the plugin version, device or platform, relevant page, expected outcome and a redacted example. For privacy and security reports, use private email and omit passwords, chat links and confidential attachments.

Coverage tool unavailable

Continue studying in ChatGPT and make a manual note of the pages covered. If the error recurs, tell support when it happened and include a short, redacted description.
