Support

Resolve a study issue

ChatGPT cannot read my PDF

Check whether text can be selected in the PDF. For scans, try an accessible text copy or paste a short excerpt you are allowed to share. Give the PDF page number and mention whether printed page numbers differ. For upload errors affecting ChatGPT itself, contact OpenAI support.

My place in a lecture was lost

Paste your saved checkpoint, specify the PDF page and section heading, and ask to resume there. Reattach the material if it is no longer accessible in the conversation.

The feedback or assessment looks wrong

Ask for the specific reasoning and have it compared with the relevant course definition or marking criteria. If you want to continue without assessment, state that explicitly.

Prepare an issue report

For a bug or incorrect academic information, record the plugin version, platform, PDF page or section, what you asked, what occurred and what you expected. For a scientific or mathematical discrepancy, include a brief permitted source excerpt or a reference and identify the specific claim.

Use a short synthetic or redacted example. Remove names, student numbers, private chat links, credentials and confidential course material. For suspected privacy or security problems, keep the report private and share only the minimum facts needed.

Incorrect references or page numbers

Include the PDF page number and printed page number if they differ, the section heading and a short permitted excerpt showing the discrepancy. For scoring or feedback, include the question, your redacted answer and the marking step you believe is wrong.

Report a security or privacy issue

Use email for responsible disclosure. Summarize the affected product or page, what you observed and when, and any potential data exposure. Keep details private while the issue is being assessed. Omit credentials, private chat links, real user data and confidential files.

Contact routes

Email is the direct support channel. Use a short subject such as Technical issue, Privacy request or Security report. For privacy requests, describe the right you want to exercise and provide enough information to locate the relevant correspondence. Verification, if needed, is handled separately.

Email the publisher at info@mkdad.de for tutoring issues, privacy requests or confidential security reports. For a ChatGPT account, upload, or platform security issue, use OpenAI Help Center. For OpenAI account data requests, use the OpenAI privacy portal.

Support correspondence is deleted 90 days after case closure, except where longer retention is legally required. See the privacy notice for the website and platform roles.
