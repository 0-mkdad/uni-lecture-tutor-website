Privacy notice

Publisher and privacy contact

Mohammad Al Mekdad, an independent developer in Germany, is responsible for the publisher’s processing described below. See the Impressum for publisher identification. For publisher privacy requests, contact info@mkdad.de.

The publisher does not collect lecture files or conversations through the skill and operates no dedicated backend or database for the tutoring workflow. The publisher uses no analytics, advertising or tracking on the site or in the skill. The publisher processes correspondence sent to the support and privacy mailbox.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages delivers these pages. Requests send network and browser information to GitHub. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its privacy statement describes its own processing, retention criteria, international transfers and privacy contact.

This site loads its styles and images from the same origin. The site code sets no cookies, uses no browser storage and includes no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or embedded third-party content. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own practices.

Conversations and lecture files

When you provide a prompt, conversation or file in ChatGPT, the platform handles that content. Uni Lecture Tutor uses instructions and reference materials within ChatGPT to guide the study workflow. It has no separate tutoring server or publisher-operated database for your lectures. OpenAI’s processing, retention, controls and international transfers depend on the product, account settings and any managed workspace arrangements.

OpenAI European privacy policy · ChatGPT data controls

When outside research is needed, the tutoring instructions call for generic, non-identifying searches through available ChatGPT tools. They prohibit including private lecture text or personal identifiers in those queries. The privacy terms of the services used through ChatGPT apply to that activity.

Support correspondence and privacy requests

When you email the publisher, the publisher handles your email address, message, technical details and any attachments you provide. The publisher uses the information to answer inquiries, investigate reported problems and handle privacy requests. Share only the minimum needed and use a redacted example.

The publisher relies on its legitimate interest in responding to inquiries, investigating technical or security reports and resolving complaints under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where responding is necessary to comply with a statutory obligation, including applicable GDPR requests, Article 6(1)(c) applies. Article 6(1)(b) applies where processing is necessary to provide a contracted service or take requested steps before contracting.

The email service used by the publisher handles delivery and storage of support correspondence. The publisher deletes support correspondence 90 days after case closure, except where longer retention is legally required.

For ChatGPT account data and platform privacy requests, use OpenAI’s privacy portal. For GitHub’s own processing, use the contact provided in its privacy statement.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your data, restrict its processing, receive portable data, object to processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. Conditions apply to individual rights, including safeguards for qualifying solely automated decisions. To exercise your rights concerning the publisher’s processing, contact info@mkdad.de. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including where you live or work or where an alleged infringement occurred.

Children and lecture material

The tutoring workflow is designed for university learners. ChatGPT’s age and permission rules apply to platform use. Avoid including personal information about minors or other people in lecture files where it is unnecessary.
