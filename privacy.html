Controller and contact

Mohammad Almekdad, an independent developer in Germany, is responsible for the project processing described here. For privacy questions or requests, email info@mkdad.de.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages delivers the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. HTTP requests can also expose browser headers, timestamps and requested paths. The site loads its assets locally and its code uses no analytics, cookies or browser storage.

The legal basis for delivering and securing the site is the publisher’s legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for GitHub’s own processing and retention criteria.

Lectures and ChatGPT

ChatGPT processes lecture files, prompts and study conversations and generates explanations. The applicable OpenAI policy, account controls and workspace arrangements govern how OpenAI handles this data. Use OpenAI’s privacy policy and privacy portal for platform data and rights requests. The project website has no file upload feature.

Optional coverage calculator

When ChatGPT invokes validate_lecture_coverage, the Cloudflare Worker receives total_pages (the PDF page count) and covered_pages (the page numbers marked covered). It counts distinct covered pages and returns the remaining count, percentage and first uncovered page, if any. The tool is read-only and stateless. Its contract requires no files, text, filenames or student identity. The publisher maintains no separate student account or progress database.

Request metadata and retention

Cloudflare hosts the endpoint for the publisher. It may process HTTP metadata such as the connecting system’s IP address, request headers, timestamps, paths, response status and infrastructure logs. The publisher relies on its legitimate interest in providing the calculator and operating it securely (Article 6(1)(f)). The calculation is performed for the request. Cloudflare may still process technical data at the infrastructure level. Retention of technical data depends on the logging settings, the provider and the purpose, such as diagnosing errors or protecting the service. See Cloudflare and Workers Logs.

Email

If you contact the publisher, the email provider handles delivery and mailbox storage. The publisher processes your address, message and any details you provide to respond, troubleshoot or handle privacy requests. The basis is legitimate interest in responding and maintaining the service (Article 6(1)(f)), or a legal obligation where applicable (Article 6(1)(c)). Correspondence is retained while needed for the case and longer where legally required or needed for a legal claim. Share a short redacted example.

International transfers

The providers may process data outside the EEA. The applicable mechanism depends on the provider, destination and terms, and may include an adequacy decision or EU standard contractual clauses. See the policies linked above and the Cloudflare DPA for details or contact the publisher to ask about applicable safeguards.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing, including on grounds relating to your situation for Article 6(1)(f). You can withdraw consent if it is the basis used. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example LDI Nordrhein-Westfalen or the authority where you live or work.

Data minimisation and student control

The numeric tool contract excludes lecture text, filenames, account identifiers, assessment answers, checkpoints and learning histories. The website has no advertising trackers or lecture upload form. The tutor requires no separate registration or publisher-operated student-progress database.

The publisher’s policy is not to sell student data or use advertising trackers in the website code. The optional numeric calculator is not a student analytics service.
