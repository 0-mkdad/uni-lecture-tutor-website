How it works

  1. Upload

    Attach a readable lecture PDF or share an excerpt in ChatGPT.

  2. Understand

    Identify the lecture’s structure and the prerequisites you need.

  3. Learn

    Work through pages, formulas and examples in order.

  4. Practice

    Answer a question independently and ask for feedback.

  5. Review

    Plan spaced practice around the topics that need more work.

  6. Resume

    Save a short checkpoint and paste it when you return.

Study tools for university courses

Explanations at your pace

Ask for a simpler explanation, a derivation or a worked example. Choose your explanation language and keep notes and terms in the course language.

Notes tied to the lecture

Follow the source in order and ask for page references. Keep source material distinct from AI-generated practice questions.

Recall and review

Practise with one question at a time, ask for hints, and plan review sessions. Use checkpoints to record your place.

Page coverage

An optional calculator counts distinct pages marked covered and finds the first page still to cover. Page coverage measures material reviewed.

Where your lecture data goes

You upload lecture material in ChatGPT, where OpenAI processes it. If ChatGPT calls the optional calculator, Cloudflare receives numeric page metadata and the technical data that accompanies an HTTP request.

Frequently asked questions

Which courses can I study?

The workflow can guide you through readable materials in a range of university subjects, including mathematics, computer science and the humanities.

Which languages can I use?

Request explanations in your preferred language and specify a separate language for notes and vocabulary if needed. English, German and Arabic are examples.

Where do my lecture files go?

Files are uploaded to ChatGPT. The project website has no upload form. The coverage calculator receives only the numeric inputs needed for its calculation.

Does the calculator save my progress?

The calculator returns counts and the next uncovered page. Save a checkpoint in your own notes if you want to take it to another session.

How reliable are the answers?

AI can misread scans, formulas or page numbers and make mistakes in reasoning. Verify important details against the course material.

How do I resume a lecture?

Paste your checkpoint and give the PDF page and heading. Reattach the source if the chat can no longer access it.

Where can I get help?

Use the support page for file troubleshooting, reporting bugs and contacting the developer.
