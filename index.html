What does the determinant tell us?

For a 2 × 2 matrix, multiply the entries on each diagonal. Subtract the second product from the first.

A = abcd det A = ad − bc

If the result is nonzero, the matrix is invertible.

More than a lecture summary

Your lectures. Your language. Your pace. A guided learning workflow built for understanding, not just memorisation.

01

Lecture intelligence

Invoke the tutor with readable lecture files to map lectures and courses, identify prerequisites, sequence topics and follow source references. Track pages and elements, distinguishing inspected, explained and unreadable content.

02

Adaptive learning

Build difficulty progressively, request graduated hints, correct misconceptions and repair specific weaknesses. Keep independent performance separate from skipped or revealed answers.

03

Multilingual education

Learn in your preferred language, with source-grounded study notes in the lecture language, contextual academic vocabulary and explanations of formulas and terminology. Distinguish subject knowledge from language proficiency.

04

Academic verification

Follow derivations and proofs step by step. Separate source material, generated examples and externally verified information. Where host execution is available, bounded helpers can check question structure, state consistency and exact rational or polynomial calculations. They do not independently verify every answer.

05

Interactive practice

Use study cards, mathematical visualisations, concept maps, diagrams, guided navigation, flashcard-style practice, quizzes and exercises where supported. Text alternatives remain available.

06

Exam preparation

Practise lecture-specific and cumulative assessments with corrective feedback, progressive hints and targeted review. Distinguish recall, application, reasoning and transfer; answer independently before revealing a solution.

07

Multiple lectures and courses

Organise a single lecture or a complete course, connect related concepts across lectures and keep track of cumulative progress.

08

Portable continuity

Save your own checkpoint, restore it in a new conversation and reattach relevant lecture files. Automatic cloud synchronisation, permanent memory and background saving are not promised.

How a study session works

Start with your course material. Work through it in order and use your answers to decide what to revisit.

  1. 01

    Share your lecture

    Attach a readable lecture PDF or paste an excerpt you have permission to share in ChatGPT.

  2. 02

    Get a study plan

    See how the material is organised, which concepts come first and what background knowledge you may need.

  3. 03

    Work through the source

    Follow the lecture in order. Ask for a definition, a formula derivation or an example and check the page reference.

  4. 04

    Try a question

    Work on one problem at a time. Ask for a hint if you are stuck and get feedback on your answer.

  5. 05

    Review and pick up later

    Use practice feedback to choose what to revisit. Save a short checkpoint with the page and next topic to resume later.

Your education. Your privacy.

Lecture files and conversations are processed in ChatGPT under applicable OpenAI policies and your settings. The publisher operates no separate student-progress database. The optional coverage calculator is designed to receive only numerical page information (total_pages and covered_pages), not lecture text, filenames, account identifiers, assessment answers or checkpoints. GitHub and Cloudflare may process technical connection data needed to deliver and secure their services.

Frequently asked questions

Is Uni Lecture Tutor available to everyone?

Public directory availability has not been confirmed here. Explore the illustrative lesson and check access in ChatGPT; access depends on your plan, region, workspace and supported interface.

Is the tutor really free?

The publisher charges no separate fee and requires no additional tutor account. ChatGPT itself may have plan requirements, usage limits and charges.

What material can I study?

Start with a readable lecture PDF, such as slides or notes exported to PDF. You can also paste an excerpt. Scans, dense formulas and mismatched slide numbers can make source interpretation harder.

Which languages can I use?

Ask for explanations in English, German, Arabic or another language you prefer. You can choose a separate language for notes and vocabulary, for example Arabic explanations with German course terms.

How can I check that I understood the lecture?

Ask for one question at a time and try to answer independently. Request a hint or feedback. Page coverage tracks the material you have worked through. Your answers show what you can recall and apply.

Can I continue in another chat?

Save a short checkpoint and paste it when you return. Include the lecture, page and heading. Reattach the source if the new chat needs it.

Who can access my lecture material?

Uploading happens in ChatGPT. OpenAI handles that material according to its applicable policies and your account or workspace settings. The privacy notice explains how the website and optional calculator handle data.

How reliable are the explanations?

AI can misread scans, formulas or page numbers and make reasoning errors. Check important details against your lecture and follow your institution’s rules on AI use.
