Fictional lecture · Page 4

What does the determinant tell us?

For a 2 × 2 matrix, multiply the entries on each diagonal. Subtract the second product from the first.

A = abcd det A = ad − bc

If the result is nonzero, the matrix is invertible.

More than a lecture summary

Your lectures. Your language. Your pace. A guided learning workflow built for understanding, not just memorisation.

No long start prompt required

Attach and submit accessible lecture files with the tutor invoked. The tutor is designed to identify the material, select the appropriate teaching profile, outline the course and start the first verified learning objective in the same response. No compulsory setup questionnaire. Explicit requests take priority; missing or unreadable sources require recovery.

Try a guided question

Illustrative data only · fictitious papers

A square matrix has determinant 0. Is it invertible? Justify your answer.

01Explain

Recall the relationship between determinant and invertibility. What condition must hold for an inverse to exist?

02Hint

Compare the determinant with zero. Which side of the invertibility condition does it satisfy?

03Show a parallel example

For a different square matrix B with det(B) = 2, the determinant is nonzero, so B is invertible.

04Reveal the solution

No. A square matrix is invertible if and only if its determinant is nonzero. Here det(A) = 0.

How a study session works

Start with your course material. Work through it in order and use your answers to decide what to revisit.

  1. 01

    Share your lecture

    Attach a readable lecture PDF or paste an excerpt you have permission to share in ChatGPT.

  2. 02

    Get a study plan

    See how the material is organised, which concepts come first and what background knowledge you may need.

  3. 03

    Work through the source

    Follow the lecture in order. Ask for a definition, a formula derivation or an example and check the page reference.

  4. 04

    Try a question

    Work on one problem at a time. Ask for a hint if you are stuck and get feedback on your answer.

  5. 05

    Review and pick up later

    Use practice feedback to choose what to revisit. Save a short checkpoint with the page and next topic to resume later.

Verified external information

Different disciplines. Different ways to reason.

Try three short, generated illustrations of the teaching approach. These are not examples extracted from an uploaded lecture or a live tutor session.

Computing

A loop visits each item of an array once. How does the number of visits grow with input length n?

Show reasoning

There are n visits; under constant work per visit, time grows linearly, O(n).

Law

A fictional rule requires both A and B. Only A is established. Can you conclude that the rule applies?

Show reasoning

No. Both conditions must be established. In real coursework, also check exceptions, jurisdiction and the applicable date.

Research methods

Two variables are correlated in an observational study. Does that alone establish causation?

Show reasoning

No. Consider confounding, reverse causation, study design and uncertainty.

The publisher’s policy is not to sell student data or use advertising trackers in the website code. The optional numeric calculator is not a student analytics service.

Frequently asked questions

Is Uni Lecture Tutor available to everyone?

Public directory availability has not been confirmed here. Explore the illustrative lesson and check access in ChatGPT; access depends on your plan, region, workspace and supported interface.

Is the tutor really free?

The publisher charges no separate fee and requires no additional tutor account. ChatGPT itself may have plan requirements, usage limits and charges.

What material can I study?

Start with a readable lecture PDF, such as slides or notes exported to PDF. You can also paste an excerpt. Scans, dense formulas and mismatched slide numbers can make source interpretation harder.

Which languages can I use?

Choose explanation, notes and exam languages separately. Notes default to the lecture language, and exam prompts to the notes language. Original terminology, symbols and units are preserved.

How can I check that I understood the lecture?

Answer independently before requesting a hint or revealing a solution. Page coverage describes material addressed, not mastery. The tutor uses observed independent performance to suggest targeted retries, review and transfer tasks.

Can I continue in another chat?

Save a portable checkpoint yourself and submit it with relevant lecture files in a new conversation. The tutor must reconcile imported history with accessible evidence before treating it as verified; inaccessible files cannot be recovered automatically.

Who can access my lecture material?

Uploading happens in ChatGPT. OpenAI handles that material according to its applicable policies and your account or workspace settings. The privacy notice explains how the website and optional calculator handle data.

How reliable are the explanations?

AI can misread scans, formulas or page numbers and make reasoning errors. Check important details against your lecture and follow your institution’s rules on AI use.

Do I need to write a start prompt?

No separate prompt is required when you invoke the tutor and submit accessible lecture files. It is designed to start the first supported objective in the same response, unless you request another task.

Can I export my study materials?

Yes, request any of the eight supported export types. Actual file generation depends on the host; copyable content is the fallback. Anki uses manual CSV import.
