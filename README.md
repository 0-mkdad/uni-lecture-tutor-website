# Uni Lecture Tutor

**Your university. Your language. Your personal AI tutor.**

Uni Lecture Tutor (public product version **1.0.0**) is an independent, multilingual university learning assistant designed for ChatGPT. Turn readable lectures into structured study sessions, build understanding, practise independently and prepare for exams.

## Development preview: 1.1.0

Past Exam Intelligence extends the lecture workflow with single- and multi-paper analysis, source-linked question families, recurrence, descriptive exam style, paper/page/question and family navigation, guided solving, parallel examples, first-error feedback, independent mock exams, adaptive transfer training and a portable exam ledger. Historical frequencies are not predictions; host acceptance and release approval remain pending. The current public 1.0.0 availability remains unchanged.

## Key features

- **Lecture intelligence:** Invoke the tutor and submit accessible lecture files; no written launch prompt is required. The workflow is designed to map lectures and begin the first supported objective in the same response. Identify prerequisites, sequence topics and follow source references. Track pages and elements, distinguishing inspected, explained and unreadable content.
- **Adaptive learning:** Build difficulty progressively, request graduated hints, correct misconceptions and repair specific weaknesses. Keep independent performance separate from skipped or revealed answers.
- **Multilingual education:** Choose explanation, source-note and exam languages separately, with contextual academic vocabulary and preserved original terminology, notation and units. Distinguish subject knowledge from language proficiency.
- **Academic verification:** Follow derivations and proofs step by step. Separate source material, generated examples and externally verified information. Where host execution is available, bounded helpers can check question structure, state consistency and exact rational or polynomial calculations. They do not independently verify every answer.
- **Interactive practice:** Use study cards, mathematical visualisations, concept maps, diagrams, guided navigation, flashcard-style practice, quizzes and exercises where supported. Text alternatives remain available.
- **Exam preparation:** Practise lecture-specific and cumulative assessments with corrective feedback, progressive hints and targeted review. Distinguish recall, application, reasoning and transfer; answer independently before revealing a solution.
- **Multiple lectures and courses:** Organise a single lecture or a complete course, connect related concepts across lectures and keep track of cumulative progress.
- **Portable continuity:** Save a portable checkpoint yourself and reattach relevant lecture files in a new conversation. Imported history must be reconciled with accessible evidence; it does not prove mastery. No automatic durable storage or cross-chat file access is promised.

## Study modes, source integrity and exports

- Guided and continuous teaching, adjustable depth, direct answers, notes, independent practice, review and practice exams are supported learner requests; not separate guaranteed UI modes.
- Five labelled categories distinguish essential knowledge, authentic examples, source-grounded notes, generated learning content and independently verified external information.
- Page and element coverage are distinct from assessment and mastery. Revealed, supported and skipped answers do not automatically prove independent mastery; review suggestions do not create automatic reminders.
- Request a study script (PDF, Markdown or DOCX), formula sheet, academic glossary, practice exam, separate solutions, Anki-compatible UTF-8 CSV, error notebook or portable JSON checkpoint. Files depend on host support; copyable formats are the fallback. No direct Anki integration or external automatic storage.

## Free use and privacy

The publisher charges no separate tutor fee and requires no additional account. Access remains subject to ChatGPT availability, plan requirements, platform terms, region, workspace settings and usage limits. OpenAI may charge for its services. Public directory availability should be checked in ChatGPT.

Lecture files and conversations are processed in ChatGPT under applicable OpenAI policies and your settings. The publisher operates no separate student-progress database. The optional coverage calculator is designed to receive only numerical page information (total_pages and covered_pages), not lecture text, filenames, account identifiers, assessment answers or checkpoints. GitHub and Cloudflare may process technical connection data needed to deliver and secure their services. Coverage is not mastery. Public research may be used when necessary or requested, with generic non-identifying queries and verified public citations. The instructions prohibit disclosure of private passages, identifiers and checkpoint contents; they are not a technical data-loss-prevention guarantee. The publisher’s policy is not to sell student data; the website code has no advertising trackers. Verify important material and follow institutional rules. This is an independent project, not an official OpenAI or university product.
